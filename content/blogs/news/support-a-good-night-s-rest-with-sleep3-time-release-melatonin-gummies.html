What Is Melatonin & How Does It Work? 

Melatonin, also referred to as the sleep hormone, is produced naturally by the brain’s pineal gland and plays a critical role in the sleep-wake cycle.

To understand how Melatonin supplements work to support a good night’s rest, it’s important to know how your body’s natural Melatonin level rises and falls throughout the night:

  1. As night approaches, your body begins producing Melatonin to promote restful sleep.
  2. For the first half of the night, your Melatonin rises slowly. 
  3. For the second half of the night, your Melatonin falls slowly.   

With a traditional sleep supplement that contains only quick-release Melatonin, your Melatonin may rise too fast, then drop off quickly as the supplement is broken down. While quick-release Melatonin could help you drift off at bedtime, it may not last through the night.

That’s why we designed Nature’s Bounty® Sleep3 Gummies to mimic the body’s natural release pattern of Melatonin.* Quick-release Melatonin releases quickly to help you fall asleep, and time-release Melatonin releases gradually over time to help you stay asleep longer.*

How Does Time-Release Melatonin Work? 

The time-release Melatonin in Nature’s Bounty® Sleep3 Gummies is coated in a special hydrogel layer. This coating protects the Melatonin from your stomach acids, preventing it from being released too early. In the meantime, our uncoated Melatonin quickly releases to help you drift off.* Once the time-release Melatonin reaches your small intestine, the coating dissolves, releasing the Melatonin gradually to help you enjoy longer-lasting sleep.*
