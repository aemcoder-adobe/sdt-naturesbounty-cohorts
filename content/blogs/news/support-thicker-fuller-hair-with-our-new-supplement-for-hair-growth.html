What’s Inside Our Hair Growth Supplement? 

Nature’s Bounty® Optimal Solutions® Hair Growth contains our Advanced Hair Complex, which includes Arginine, Silicon and Biotin—that work to nourish your follicles and promote growth of thicker, fuller hair.*

Biotin, a water-soluble B vitamin, plays a role in keratin production and is important for healthy hair.* While Biotin can be found in many foods we eat, such as eggs, fish, meat, seeds, and nuts, taking a Biotin supplement can help provide extra support if you don’t get enough of this important nutrient in your diet.

Silicon is a trace element that is found in healthy hair, skin, and nails. While you can get this nutrient from foods such as grain, fish, meat, and eggs, Silicon may also be taken as a supplement to support healthy hair.*
