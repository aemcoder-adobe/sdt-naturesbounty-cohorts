What Is Berberine? Get the Facts Behind This Popular Supplement

Learn about Berberine and the potential benefits of adding this supplement to your wellness routine.

Have you discovered our latest product, Nature’s Bounty® Berberine? Berberine is an herbal supplement derived from a compound found in the bark of the Berberine plant. Berberine has been traditionally used for centuries and is found to have several key health benefits, including supporting your heart and cardiovascular system.* If you’re new to this popular herbal ingredient, here’s why you should consider making it part of your vitamin routine.

The Health Benefits of Berberine

Wondering why so many people are trying this traditional herbal supplement? Berberine is known to have valuable health benefits, including:

  • Supporting heart and cardiovascular health* 
  • Helping to maintain healthy cholesterol, triglyceride and blood sugar levels already in the normal range* 

If you are looking for a supplement to help support your heart, cardiovascular health and maintain blood sugar levels already within a normal range, ask your doctor if adding Berberine to your vitamin routine could be right for your wellness goals.*

About Nature’s Bounty® Berberine

Each serving of Nature’s Bounty® Berberine contains 1,000 mg of Berberine derived from the bark of the Berberis aristata plant.
