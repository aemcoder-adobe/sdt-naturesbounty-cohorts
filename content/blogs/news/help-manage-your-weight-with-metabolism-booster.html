What Is Metabolism—And How Can You Can Support Yours?  

Metabolism is the chemical process your body uses to turn the food and drinks you consume into cellular energy. During this process, calories mix with oxygen to create the cellular energy your body needs. Your metabolism is important for many critical bodily functions, including:

  • Breathing 
  • Circulating blood 
  • Digesting food 
  • Growing and repairing cells 
  • Managing hormone levels 
  • Regulating body temperature 

Metabolism also plays a role in weight management. Your basal metabolic rate (BMR) is the minimum number of calories your body needs to function at rest. Someone with a fast metabolism (or high BMR) burns more calories at rest compared to someone with a slow metabolism (or low BMR).

BMR varies from person to person and is impacted by factors such as age, weight, sex, and muscle mass. For instance, men tend to have a faster metabolism than women, since they often have more muscle mass, less body fat, and larger bones. As you get older, your metabolism may also slow down as you lose muscle mass.

However, there may be a few ways to maintain a healthy metabolism and support metabolic health:

  • Avoid skipping meals 
  • Choose healthy foods, including fruits and veggies, lean protein, and healthy fats and carbohydrates 
  • Build muscle through strength and resistance training
