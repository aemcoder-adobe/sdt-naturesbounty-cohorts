Benefits of Fish Oil

Nature's Bounty® Fish Oil contains Omega-3 fatty acids including EPA and DHA which help support and maintain the health of your cardiovascular and circulatory system.*

Fish Oil is a “good” fat that is derived from fish. Believe it or not, this versatile supplement provides a number of health benefits.* Nature's Bounty® Fish Oil contains Omega-3 fatty acids including EPA and DHA which help support and maintain the health of your cardiovascular and circulatory system.* Here are some of the benefits of fish oil:

​

May Reduce Risk of Coronary Heart Disease:
Coronary heart disease occurs in the heart’s major blood vessels. Supportive but not conclusive research shows that consumption of EPA and DHA Omega-3 fatty acids may reduce the risk of coronary heart disease. See supplement facts panel for omega-3 content. In addition to supplementing with fish oil, it is important to regularly exercise and consume a healthy diet.*

Supports Triglycerides Within a Normal Range*:
Triglycerides are an important way to measure heart health. They indicate the amount of fat found in your blood, and should be kept within a normal range for a healthy heart. Omega-3 fatty acids found in fish oil are considered “good” fats that help maintain triglyceride levels already within a normal range.**

Supports Overall Wellness:*
In addition to supporting cardiovascular health, omega-3 fatty acid’s can help with other areas of wellness.* These healthy fats support metabolic health and can help to support healthy skin.* Consuming a diet rich in salmon, walnuts and flax-seed oil is another great way to incorporate omega-3’s while taking a daily fish oil supplement!

​

No matter what aspect of your health you’re looking to support, there are plenty of reasons to try Nature’s Bounty® Fish Oil today.
