Hair, Skin and Beauty Hacks Every Woman Needs to Know

Save time and frustration with these life-changing beauty hacks that every woman should know.

When it comes to supporting your natural beauty from within, we’ve got you covered. Our Hair, Skin and Nails softgels and gummies provide you with the nutrients needed to support luscious hair, vibrant skin and healthy nails.*

However, we understand that sometimes we all need a quick fix when it comes to our appearance. Save time and frustration with these life-changing beauty hacks that every woman should know:

​

Hair Beauty Hacks:

  • Need a quick fix for frizzy hair? Rub a small amount of hand lotion through your strands.
  • Make your bobby pins last longer by spraying them with hairspray before styling.
  • Dry shampoo is a great way to keep your hair looking fresh between washes. If you run out, baby powder works as an alternative.
  • To tame strands of hair affected by static, run a dryer sheet through them.

Skin Beauty Hacks:

  • Running out of makeup remover? Coconut oil is the perfect solution.
  • If you have a red blemish, use a green concealer to correct your skin color.
  • When your tan fades and your foundation is too dark, adjust the color by mixing it with some moisturizer.
  • Need a quick fix for a pimple? Applying some whitening toothpaste to the area overnight can help clear it up.

​

Nail Beauty Hacks:

  • If you don’t have time to let your nails completely dry, speed up the process by spritzing your polish with PAM cooking spray and wait only 20 seconds.
  • If you’ve ever removed your red manicure only to be left with slightly stained nails, remove the excess color by scrubbing your fingertips in whitening toothpaste.
  • Extend your manicure’s lifespan by applying white vinegar to your nails prior to painting them.
  • Repair damaged cuticles by mixing 3 tablespoons of lotion with 1 tablespoon of olive oil and microwaving for 30 seconds. Add the mixture to clean nail beds and cover with gloves before bed for revitalized cuticles in the morning.
