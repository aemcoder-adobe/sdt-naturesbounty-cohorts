Homemade Coconut Eye-Cream

Coconut Oil is a hydrating agent that helps with the appearance of skin. Try our recipe for our homemade coconut-eye cream that will nourish the skin around your eye and leave you feeling rejuvenated!

Ingredients:

  • 1/2 cup coconut oil
  • 1/2 cup Pure Sweet almond oil
  • 1/2 cup Nature's Bounty Vitamin E oil

Instructions:

  1. Combine all ingredients in a bowl, and pour into a glass jar with a cover.
  2. Use a cotton ball to apply it under the eyes and massage gently every night before bed.
