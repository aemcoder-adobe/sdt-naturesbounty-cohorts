What Are Probiotics?

You may have heard of them before, but what exactly are probiotics?

Probiotics are the good bacteria found in your body that support healthy digestion.*

Yes, “good” bacteria does exist, and there are billions of them found in your gut. Their role is to maintain digestive function and support immune health.* However, there are a variety of reasons why you might need to introduce more friendly bacteria, such as a result of external factors like diet. If this occurs, supplementing with probiotics works to replace them by introducing them to your digestive tract.* In addition to supporting your body’s natural digestive process, they also support immune function.*
