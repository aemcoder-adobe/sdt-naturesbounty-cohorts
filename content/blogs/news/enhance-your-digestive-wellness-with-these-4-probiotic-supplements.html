First, a review: what are probiotics?  
Probiotics have garnered significant attention in the realm of health and wellness due to their potential benefits. These live microorganisms play a vital role in supporting digestive health and overall well-being. Probiotics, consumed in adequate amounts, can positively influence our gut microbiome, promoting a balanced and diverse community of bacteria.
