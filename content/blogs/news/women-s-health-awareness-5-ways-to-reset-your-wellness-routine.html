5 Ways to Check in on Your Wellness Routine 

Every woman deserves to flourish and do what feels right for her body. If your current wellness routine isn’t cutting it, try these five easy adjustments:

  1. Love the way you move.

Workouts don’t have to be grueling to be beneficial. There are countless fun ways to meet the current fitness recommendations for adults, including dancing, playing tennis, hiking with a friend, or even beautifying your backyard. So, if you’re not excited about your current fitness routine, don’t be afraid to ditch the gym and try something new.

  1. Nourish your body. 

Food is so much more than fuel. It’s about memories, traditions, and gathering with loved ones to enjoy a wholesome meal. And with so many nutritious, delicious recipes available, you don’t have to trade enjoyment for nutrition. Simple tips like adding a few big handfuls of leafy greens to your favorite mac-and-cheese recipe or finding creative ways to “eat the rainbow” every day can make healthful eating a pleasure.

  1. Refresh your supplement routine.

Many of us have a supplement cabinet overflowing with bottles. But simplifying your vitamin routine can make it easier to remember to take the essentials. We recommend starting fresh with Women’s Multivitamin Gummies, a unique formula created for women, by women to provide your body with over ten key nutrients every day. These delightful raspberry and mixed-berry gummies recently won a coveted “Best Taste” award, so we know you’ll love adding them to your routine!

  1. Connect with other women.

It may sound obvious, but sharing your wellness journey with others can transform a solitary experience into a joyful and empowering community. Whether it’s joining a walking group, planning an intergenerational retreat or calling a supportive friend, reaching out to other women is a great way to get a gut check—and a reminder that you are amazing just as you are!
