Celebrate Women’s History Month with the Inspiring Women at Nature’s Bounty®

March is Women’s History Month—a time to honor women’s remarkable accomplishments, past and present!

To celebrate Women's History Month, we asked some of the many amazing women at Nature’s Bounty® to share their insights on leadership, inspiration, and the advice they would give their younger selves. We hope you enjoy meeting the minds behind our brand and learning more about their commitment to promoting women’s health, wellness, and opportunities to shine.

Meet the Women Who Shine at Nature’s Bounty®    

Jaime, Senior Brand Manager

What woman in your life inspires you the most?

“My mom is the most inspiring person to me and has been a guiding light my entire life. She shines in every way and, in her 70s, is truly living her best life. Mom has an active lifestyle, is loved by her family and many friends, and is living proof that age is just a number. She’s also a true believer in living well and takes her Hair Growth and Metabolism Booster every day!”

Jackie, Senior Brand Manager

What inspires you to be a leader?

“I have three young boys. It is important to me that they see me work hard to make a meaningful impact on people’s lives every day!”

Rachel, Assistant Brand Manager

What core values are most important to you?

“Being kind to myself and others. Staying curious and open-minded in my personal and professional life. Prioritizing self-care.”

Kass, Marketing Communications Manager

What core values do you strive to live by every day?

“Respect and integrity. Respect everyone, no matter their status in life or how different their views are from yours. I believe integrity builds trust. Do what you say you’re going to do and if you can’t do it, have an honest conversation.”

Laura, Marketing Communications Specialist

What advice would you give your younger self?

“Don’t give in to the pressure to conform to society’s predefined timeline. I’d reassure my younger self that life unfolds differently for everyone and that deviating from conventional paths is perfectly acceptable. Embrace the freedom to do things out of order, which allows for personal growth and a more authentic, fulfilling experience. The beauty of life lies in its unpredictability!”
