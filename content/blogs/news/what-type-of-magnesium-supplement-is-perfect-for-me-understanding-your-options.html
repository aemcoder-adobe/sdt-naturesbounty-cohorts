What Type of Magnesium Supplement Is Perfect for Me? Understanding Your Options

Learn about the different types of Magnesium and the potential benefits of adding a Magnesium supplement to your wellness routine.

Magnesium is an essential mineral that plays a role in many areas of health and wellness, including supporting muscle and nerve function as well as heart health.* Among other benefits, Magnesium is critical for bone health and helps to maintain bone mineralization.* At higher doses, Magnesium may even help with sleep and occasional stress or anxiety.*

However, many Americans don’t get enough Magnesium through diet alone, according to the National Health and Nutritional Examination Survey (NHANES). Magnesium supplements can help increase your Magnesium intake, but with so many options to choose from, finding the right product for your needs can be overwhelming. Fear not—this article will educate you on some of the types of Magnesium commonly found in dietary supplements, so you can select a Magnesium supplement that’s perfect for you. However, before you take any supplement, its advisable to talk to your doctor first to ensure it’s the right supplement for you.

Types of Magnesium 

Magnesium comes in many different forms. When shopping for a Magnesium supplement, consider these types of Magnesium:

Magnesium Glycinate is a Magnesium salt of the amino acid Glycine. It is a highly bioavailable form of magnesium that your body can absorb easily and use effectively.* Like other forms of Magnesium, Magnesium Glycinate helps to support bone and muscle function.*

Magnesium Benefits 

Your body needs Magnesium to stay healthy. This essential mineral is a component of over 200 enzymes in the body.* It also plays a role in everything from supporting heart health to making protein, bone, and DNA.* Depending on the form or dose, the benefits of taking a Magnesium supplement may include:

  • Supporting bone health and maintaining proper bone mineralization*  
  • Supporting healthy muscle contractions and nerve impulses* 
  • Supporting heart health* 
  • Helping with occasional stress or anxiety (at 300mg of Magnesium)*  
  • Supporting a calm, relaxed mood* 
  • Supporting sleep (at 500 mg of Magnesium)* 
  • And other potential benefits 

Not getting enough Magnesium in your diet can lead to a deficiency that may cause health problems which is why it is important to keep up on your intake of this important mineral.

How Much Magnesium Do I Need? 

According to the National Institutes of Health, the amount of Magnesium you need to be healthy depends on your age and gender. For adults, the daily recommended intake is 400–420 mg for men and 310–320 mg for women. Pregnant women should get 350–360 mg daily.

If you don’t get enough Magnesium from the foods you eat, taking a Magnesium supplement can help you meet the daily recommended intake of this important nutrient. For sleep support with continued use, you may need a supplement containing a higher amount of Magnesium, such as Nature’s Bounty® Magnesium 500 mg tablets.* Consult with your doctor before adding a Magnesium supplement to your routine.

How to Get Enough Magnesium 

Magnesium is found naturally in many healthy foods, including:

  • Nuts, seeds, and legumes 
  • Whole grains 
  • Leafy greens, such as spinach 
  • Fortified foods, such as fortified breakfast cereals 
  • Milk and yogurt 

To get enough Magnesium in your diet, it’s best to eat varied and nutritious meals. But if you’re not meeting your Magnesium needs through diet alone, taking a Magnesium supplement can help bridge the gap.

Which Magnesium Supplement Is Perfect for Me? 

With a variety of options to choose from, it’s important to remember that one form or type of Magnesium isn’t necessarily better than another. However, various supplements may have different doses, features, and benefits. Be sure to understand the uses of each supplement before deciding which one fits your needs, and consult with a doctor to find out if you could benefit from supplementing with Magnesium.
