Digestive Probiotic 10

By Natalie Meriwether, BSN

Digestive health plays an important role in our overall well-being as it affects many aspects of our health. From our ability to absorb nutrients to the strength of our immune system, a healthy digestive system is essential for maintaining general health. Our digestive system is so interconnected with the rest of our body even laughing with friends can improve digestion. Ensuring your digestion is functioning at its’ best will set you up for success on your wellness journey.

It's In Your Nature to Thrive

Your body’s intricate design and the power of the gut microbiome come together to create a harmonious and bountiful system from within. From the moment food enters your mouth to its journey through the digestive tract, your body is already equipped with an incredible mechanism that allows for nutrient absorption, balance within the gut microbiome, and waste elimination.

Within your digestive tract, a vast community of millions of bacteria naturally exists. These bacteria can be classified as either beneficial or harmful, commonly referred to as "good" and "bad" bacteria.

While our bountiful bodies do their best to keep the peace between the two, sometimes the bad outweighs the good. Certain factors such as poor diet, stress, and other environmental elements can cause disruptions within our system. When the balance between the good and harmful bacteria is thrown off, we can experience numerous digestive discomforts.

How can probiotics help my digestion?

Probiotics, which are live bacteria, play a crucial role in maintaining a healthy balance of these bacteria. They work to support the growth and proliferation of the beneficial bacteria in your gut, aiding in the maintenance of a harmonious microbial environment. By introducing probiotics into your system, you can help foster the flourishing of the "good" bacteria, promoting digestive health and overall well-being.*
