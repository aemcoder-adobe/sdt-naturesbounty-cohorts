Beauty Tips You'll Thank Us For Later

Look and feel your best this holiday season with these beauty tips to be thankful for.

With the holiday season upon us, you deserve to look and feel your absolute best. Food, alcohol, lack of sleep, and stress can throw your beauty routine completely out of whack. With Thanksgiving just around the corner, here are some beauty tips that you’ll thank us later for:

Pre-Thanksgiving Routine:

Thanksgiving is centered around family, gratitude, and most predominantly: food. Before feasting on your favorites, try establishing a pre-holiday routine to prepare your body for what’s coming. This includes drinking extra water, working out, sipping herbal tea and taking your vitamins. If you take care of your body before-hand you’ll look and feel better, and it will be easier to stick with your wellness routine through the whole holiday season.

De-bloat Your Face:

After your large Thanksgiving meal, you may notice puffiness around your face. The extra sodium can create bloating that may show-up under your eyes or on your cheeks. As soon as you wakeup, try putting a cold compress over your eyes along with a cold wash cloth. Continue to drink water with lemon all day to rehydrate.

Treat Your Breakouts:

If you notice a breakout right after your big feast, treat it as soon as possible. Using a salicylic acid spot treatment can help reduce inflammation. If you don’t notice anything the night of Thanksgiving, wash your face anyway to try and prevent acne from forming.

Get your Beauty Rest:

Sleep is a powerful tool when it comes to your beauty routine. If you notice bags under your eyes, lack of quality rest is the culprit. Make sure you’re getting enough sleep each night so you can look your best for all upcoming festivities.
