Probiotic Rich Breakfast Bowl

Probiotics provide support to your digestive health and immune system.* In addition to supplementing with one of our Digestive Health products, try incorporating this delicious probiotic-rich breakfast bowl into your morning routine! Since Greek yogurt contains live active cultures, you’ll be supporting your digestion while eating a balanced breakfast.*

Ingredients:

  • ¼ cup granola
  • 1 cup nonfat Greek Yogurt
  • 1 tsp honey
  • ½ tsp cinnamon
  • 1/2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/8 cup sliced strawberries
  • 1/8 cup blueberries

Directions:

  1. Mix Greek Yogurt, honey, cinnamon, and vanilla extract in a medium sized bowl
  2. Add granola and stir
  3. Top with strawberries, blueberries, and any other desired toppings
  4. Enjoy!
