Breakfast Smoothie

405 Calories / 5g Fat / 71g Carbohydrates / 26g Protein

  • 2 Scoops Optimal Solutions® Vanilla Complete Protein & Vitamin Shake Mix
  • ½ Cup Skim Milk or Water
  • 1 Banana
  • ½ Cup Strawberries
  • ½ Cup Oatmeal
  • 1 Cup Ice

Blend all ingredients in blender
