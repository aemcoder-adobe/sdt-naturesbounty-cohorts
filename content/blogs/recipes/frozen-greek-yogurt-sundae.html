Frozen Greek Yogurt Sundae

213 Calories / 1g Fat / 22g Carbohydrates / 30g Protein

Ingredients:

  • 1 Scoop Optimal Solutions® Vanilla Complete Protein & Vitamin Shake Mix
  • 8oz non-fat plain Greek Yogurt
  • 2 TBS Stevia
  • 1 TBS of marshmallows
  • Sprinkles (to garnish)

Directions:

  1. Mix 1 scoop of protein powder into your Greek yogurt.
  2. Next, add 2 TBS of stevia (this can vary depending on your preference and how sweet you like it).
  3. Place mixture in the freezer for 30-40 minutes.
  4. Top with marshmallows, sprinkles, and whatever other toppings you would like!
