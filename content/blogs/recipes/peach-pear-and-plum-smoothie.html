Peach, Pear & Plum Smoothie

  • 2 Scoops Optimal Solutions® Vanilla Complete Protein & Vitamin Shake Mix
  • 1 ½ Cups Skim Milk or Water
  • ½ Cup Frozen Peaches
  • ¼ Cup Chopped Pears
  • ¼ Cup Chopped Plums
  • 1 Cup Ice

Blend all ingredients in blender
