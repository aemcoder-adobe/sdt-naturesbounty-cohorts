Peanut Butter and Jelly Protein Shake

330 Calories / 10g Fat / 37g Carbohydrates / 29g Protein

  • 2 Scoops Optimal Solutions® Vanilla Complete Protein & Vitamin Shake Mix
  • ½ Cup Frozen Strawberries
  • 1 Cup Skim Milk
  • 1 Tbs Natural Peanut Butter
  • 1 Cup Ice

Combine all ingredients in blender and enjoy!
