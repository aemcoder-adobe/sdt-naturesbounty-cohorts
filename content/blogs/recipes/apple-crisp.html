Apple Crisp

400 Calories / 14g Fat / 46g Carbohydrates / 29g Protein

  • 2 Scoops Optimal Solutions® complete-protein-and-vitamin-shake-mix-16-oz-vanilla-powder
  • 1 ½ Cups Skim Milk or Water
  • 1 – 1 ½ Cups Chopped Apples (or ½ Cup applesauce – unsweetened)
  • ¼ Cup Almonds
  • ½ tsp Cinnamon
  • 1 Cup Ice

Blend all ingredients in blender
