Chocolate Peanut Butter Protein Oats

365 calories / 12g Fat / 45g Carbohydrates / 24g Protein

Ingredients:

  • 2 Scoops Optimal Solutions® Chocolate Complete Protein & Vitamin Shake Mix
  • ½ Cup Oatmeal (measured dry)
  • 1 Tbs Natural Peanut Butter
  • 1 Tsp Stevia
  • 1/8 tsp salt

Directions:

  1. Add water to oatmeal and cook on high for 1-3 minutes (cook time will vary)
  2. Once oatmeal is finished cooking, add 2 scoops of protein, 1 tbs of natural peanut butter, stevia and salt
  3. Stir thoroughly. Let cool for at least 1 minute before eating
