Pina Colada Protein Shake

247 Calories / 2g Fat / 37g Carbohydrates / 24g Protein

  • 2 Scoops Optimal Solutions® Vanilla Complete Protein & Vitamin Shake Mix
  • ½ Cup Frozen Pineapple
  • 1 Cup Light Coconut Milk or Skim Milk
  • 1 Cup Ice

Combine all ingredients in blender and enjoy!
