Protein French Toast

Amount Per Serving (2 slices)
(Note: May vary depending on what type of bread you use.)

Ingredients:

  • 1 Scoop Optimal Solutions® Vanilla Complete Protein & Vitamin Shake Mix
  • 2 Slices of Light Whole Wheat Bread
  • 2 Tbs Unsweetened Almond Milk
  • 3 Large Egg Whites (100g)
  • 1 Tsp Cinnamon
  • 1 Tsp Stevia
  • Non-stick Spray

Toppings: (optional)

  • Sugar Free Syrup
  • Fat-free Cool Whip
  • Blueberries

Directions:

  1. Pre-heat griddle and spray with non-stick oil.
  2. In a large bowl, whisk together 1 scoop Optimal Solutions® Vanilla Complete Protein & Vitamin Shake Mix, 3 large egg whites, 2 Tbs unsweetened vanilla almond milk and 1 tsp Stevia and Cinnamon.
  3. Soak each slice of whole wheat bread in the mixture until it is completely absorbed by each side of the bread.
  4. Place the soaked bread on the skillet and cook until the bottoms start to appear golden.
  5. Flip the French toast, and cook thoroughly.
  6. Add your desired toppings, and enjoy!
