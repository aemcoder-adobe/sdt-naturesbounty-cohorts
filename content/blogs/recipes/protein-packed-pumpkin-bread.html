Protein Packed Pumpkin Bread

Amount Per Serving (Serves 8):

Dry Ingredients:

  • 1 Cup of Oatmeal (grinded into oat flour)
  • 3 Scoops Nature's Bounty® Complete Protein & Vitamin Shake Mix
  • 1 Tsp Baking Powder
  • 1 Tsp Cinnamon
  • 1 Tsp Pumpkin Pie Spice
  • 1/4 Cup Dark Chocolate Chips
  • Stevia to Taste

Wet Ingredients:

  • 1/2 Cup Pumpkin PurÃ©e
  • 1/4 Cup Plain Non-Fat Greek Yogurt
  • 2 Egg Whites

Instructions: Preheat oven to 350. In a large bowl combine dry ingredients together with the exception of the chocolate chips. Then add in the wet ones and mix thoroughly. Fold in the chocolate chips. Spray a bread loaf pan and bake on 350 for 30-40 min, watching closely.
