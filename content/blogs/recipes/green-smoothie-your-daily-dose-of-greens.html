Green Smoothie - Your Daily Dose of Greens!

In addition to the digestive health support you get from your daily Probiotic 10, go green every once in a while with our delicious and health-conscious probiotic green smoothie!*

Ingredients:

  • 1 cup of spinach
  • 1 cup of Nonfat Greek Yogurt
  • 1 cup low-fat milk
  • 1 medium banana
  • 1 tbs honey
  • Handful of ice

Blend together and enjoy!
