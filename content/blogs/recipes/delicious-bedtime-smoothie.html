Delicious Bedtime Smoothie

Getting enough sleep is a critical component of overall wellness. Unfortunately, many healthy adults fail to recognize just how important it is to obtain at least 8 hours of quality rest each night. Our Melatonin products support sound sleep, and are a great option for those who experience occasional sleeplessness. * In addition to supplementing with Melatonin, there are a variety of foods that can be consumed before bed to help you relax and set the mood for a sound slumber.* This delicious Bedtime Smoothie contains Chamomile tea, a popular drink that has been traditionally used for relaxation.

Cherry-Chamomile Bedtime Smoothie:

Ingredients:
 • 1 Cup Low-Fat Milk
• 2 Chamomile Tea Bags
• 1 Tbsp Honey
• 1/3 cup Old-Fashioned Oatmeal
• 1 cup cherries
• 1/3 cup Non-fat Greek Yogurt
• 1 Tbsp Almond Butter
• Handful of Ice

Directions:
 1. Heat milk and tea bags in microwave. Cover and let tea seep for 10 minutes.
2. Once cooled, combine milk, tea, honey, oatmeal, cherries, yogurt and almond butter in large blender.
3. Add a handful of ice, blend on high and enjoy this delicious smoothie directly before bed.
