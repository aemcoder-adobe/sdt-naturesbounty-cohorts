Vegan Chocolate Peanut Butter Vitamin Smoothie

Decadent chocolate protein pairs perfectly with peanut butter in this delicious vegan smoothie recipe. Featuring new Nature’s Bounty® Complete Plant Protein & Vitamin Decadent Chocolate Shake Mix, a unique plant-based formula packed with 15 key vitamins and minerals, this shake offers complete and well-rounded nutrition to support key areas of your health. Our formula contains Vitamin C for antioxidant support in addition to immune and skin health, fiber and probiotics for digestive health, and B-Vitamins to provide energy metabolism support.* This innovative protein blend is also made from pea, hemp, and rice, making it 100% vegan and 100% delicious. It also contains calcium and vitamin D to promote healthy bones, biotin to support gorgeous hair, skin, and nails and electrolytes for hydration support.* Plus, it packs a powerful punch with 15g of plant-based protein per serving!

Although our Plant Protein & Vitamin Decadent Chocolate Shake Mix tastes great just mixed with cold water or your favorite dairy-free milk , shake things up with the below Vegan Chocolate Peanut Butter Smoothie recipe!

Ingredients:

  • 1 1/2 cups unsweetened vanilla almond milk
  • 1 scoop of Nature’s Bounty® Complete Plant Protein & Vitamin Shake Mix in Decadent Chocolate
  • 1 large banana (frozen)
  • 2 tbs peanut butter
  • 1/2 cup ice

Directions:

  • Combine 1 cup of almond milk and frozen banana into blender and blend until smooth
  • Add protein powder, peanut butter, and rest of almond milk. Blend on high.
  • Add ice (and any additional almond milk as needed) until reaching desired consistency.
  • Pour into large glass and enjoy!
