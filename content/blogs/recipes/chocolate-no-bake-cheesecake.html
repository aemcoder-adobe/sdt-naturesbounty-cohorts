Chocolate No-Bake Cheesecake

Amount Per Serving (makes 8 slices)
241 Calories / 10g Fat / 24g Carbohydrates / 13g protein

Filling:

  • 4½ Scoops Optimal Solutions® Chocolate Complete Protein & Vitamin Shake Mix
  • 2 8 oz. Packages Low Fat Cream Cheese
  • 1 Tbs Stevia Sweetener
  • ½ Cup + 2 Tbs Skim Milk
  • 1 tsp Vanilla Extract

Crust:

  • 1 ¼ Cup Graham Cracker Crumbs
  • 1 Tbs Stevia Sweetener
  • 5 Tbs Non-Trans Margarine

Directions:

  1. In mixing bowl, combine stevia and graham cracker crumbs
  2. Drizzle in melted margarine while mixing
  3. Tamp mixture into 9” pie shell with back of wooden spoon. Refrigerate for 15 min
  4. Mix Chocolate Protein & Vitamin Shake Mix with cream cheese until smooth, add milk
  5. Mix in vanilla until batter is smooth
  6. Pour into prepared pie shell
  7. Refrigerate at least 4 hours
