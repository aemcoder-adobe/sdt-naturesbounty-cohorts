Protein “S’mores” Bowl

337 Calories / 6g Fat / 41g Carbohydrates / 35g Protein

Ingredients:

  • 2 Scoops Optimal Solutions® Vanilla or Chocolate Complete Protein & Vitamin Shake Mix
  • 6 oz Non-Fat Plain Greek Yogurt
  • 1 Tbs Dark Chocolate chips
  • 1 Tbs Mini Marshmallows
  • 1 Square Low-Fat Graham Crackers
  • Stevia to taste (optional)

Directions:

  1. In a small bowl, combine Greek yogurt and your choice of chocolate or vanilla protein powder
  2. Break up graham cracker, add chocolate chips and marshmallows
