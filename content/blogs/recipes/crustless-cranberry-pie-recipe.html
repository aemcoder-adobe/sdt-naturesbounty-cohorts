Ingredients:

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup granulated sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1⁄4 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups cranberries (fresh or frozen)
  • 1⁄2 cup chopped walnuts
  • 1 stick unsalted butter, melted
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 teaspoon almond extract
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract

Directions:

  1. Preheat oven to 350 degrees.
  2. Spray a 9-inch round pie pan with cooking spray and set aside.
  3. In a large bowl, whisk together sugar, cinnamon, sugar, and salt until thoroughly combined
  4. Whisk in melted butter, eggs, vanilla and almond extract.
  5. Stir in cranberries and walnuts.
  6. Spread the batter into the prepared pan. Sprinkle some extra cranberries over the top for garnish.
  7. Bake at 350 degrees for 40 minutes, or until a wooden pick inserted near the center comes out clean.
  8. Serve warm and top with vanilla ice cream and whipped cream for an even more delicious dessert.
